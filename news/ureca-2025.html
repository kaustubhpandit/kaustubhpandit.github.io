Provost's 2025 Summer UReCA Fellowship

I received the Provost's UReCA Fellowship this summer to work on something that has been a real focus of my research: figuring out whether a kidney is viable for transplant without putting the patient through a biopsy.

The approach combines photoacoustic imaging with optical coherence tomography to detect fibrosis — essentially scarring that signals how healthy the organ is — before it ever gets transplanted. Right now, clinicians often have to make those calls with limited information and under serious time pressure. The goal is to give them a faster, clearer picture so more usable organs actually reach patients who need them.
